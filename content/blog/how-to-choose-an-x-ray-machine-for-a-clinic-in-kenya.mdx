## 1. Power Supply & Generator Capacity Requirements

Before signing a purchase order for imaging equipment in Kenya, evaluating your facility’s electrical infrastructure is vital:

- **Power Generator Rating (kW):** For standard general radiography in outpatient clinics, a **32kW to 50kW high-frequency generator** is typically optimal. High-frequency generators deliver consistent dose output while minimizing exposure times.
- **Single-Phase vs. Three-Phase Power:** Many clinic locations in Nairobi, Rongai, and regional towns operate on single-phase mains supply. If three-phase supply is unreliable, consider **battery-assisted or capacitor-discharge X-ray systems** that charge from standard mains power and deliver full power during exposure.

> [!TIP]
> Always pair digital imaging equipment with a dedicated online UPS and automatic voltage regulator (AVR) to protect sensitive digital flat-panel detectors (FPD) from voltage fluctuations.

## 3. Regulatory Compliance & Radiation Protection (KNRA Standards)

In Kenya, operating diagnostic imaging equipment requires strict regulatory adherence:

1. **Kenyan Nuclear Regulatory Authority (KNRA)** licensing and facility registration.
2. **Radiation Shielding Certification:** Lead-lined walls, lead glass observation windows, and lead aprons.
3. **Personal Dosimetry (TLD Badges):** Mandatory monitoring for radiography personnel.

Our team at Medwise assists facilities with pre-purchase room layout designs and radiation safety planning to ensure seamless compliance during installation.

## 4. Total Cost of Ownership: Beyond the Initial Purchase Price

When budgeting for medical equipment in Kenya, factor in the entire lifecycle expense:

- **After-Sales Service & Preventive Maintenance:** Does your contract include periodic calibration and tube output testing?
- **Spare Parts Availability:** Are replacement cables, tubes, and boards stocked locally in Nairobi or must they be imported on emergency lead times?
- **Staff Training:** Are your radiographers trained on technical positioning and DICOM/PACS software integration?
