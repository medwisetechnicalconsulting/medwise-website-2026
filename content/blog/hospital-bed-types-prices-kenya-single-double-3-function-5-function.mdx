## 1. Single-Crank Manual Hospital Bed

The single-crank bed is an entry-level, cost-effective manual bed designed for basic patient positioning.

![Single Crank Hospital Bed with Mattress and IV Pole in Kenya](/images/blog/single-crank-hospital-bed-kenya.jpg)

*   **Function:** Single manual crank located at the foot-end controls backrest elevation ($0^\circ$ to $75^\circ$). Fixed, flat leg section.
*   **Applications:** Level 2 & 3 clinics, dispensaries, emergency observation rooms, and basic home care.
*   **Key Features:** ABS detachable head/foot boards, powder-coated steel frame, collapsible aluminium guardrails, and 4 swivel castors with individual brakes.
*   **Limitations:** Lacks knee-rest elevation; not recommended for long-term bedridden patients (exceeding 2 weeks).

## 2. Double-Crank Manual Hospital Bed (Most Popular in Kenya)

The double-crank bed is the most widely adopted bed in Kenyan private clinics and Ministry of Health (MOH) hospital wards due to its balance of price, comfort, and durability.

![Double Crank ABS Hospital Bed in Kenya](/images/blog/double-crank-hospital-bed-kenya.jpg)

*   **Function:** Dual independent manual cranks:
    *   **Crank 1:** Backrest adjustment ($0^\circ$ to $75^\circ$)
    *   **Crank 2:** Knee-rest/leg elevation ($0^\circ$ to $45^\circ$)
*   **Applications:** General wards in Level 3–4 hospitals, private nursing homes, and post-operative recovery rooms.
*   **Key Features:** High-impact ABS head/foot boards, fold-down aluminium side rails, integrated IV pole sockets, and 125mm heavy-duty castors.

## 3. Three-Crank Manual Hospital Bed (Hi-Lo Adjustment)

A 3-crank manual bed offers full body positioning along with vertical height adjustment (Hi-Lo), reducing back strain for nursing staff during patient transfers.

![Three Function Manual Hi-Lo Hospital Bed Kenya](/images/blog/3-crank-manual-hospital-bed-kenya.jpg)

*   **Function:** Three manual mechanical cranks:
    *   **Crank 1:** Backrest elevation
    *   **Crank 2:** Knee-rest elevation
    *   **Crank 3:** Overall bed height adjustment (Up / Down)
*   **Applications:** High-dependency wards, post-surgical recovery, and facilities seeking ICU-style mechanics without electrical dependence.
*   **Key Features:** Heavy-duty load capacity (up to 250 kg), split ABS or collapsible side rails, and central-brake castor options.

## 5. Five-Function Electric ICU Bed

The gold standard for critical care units, high-dependency units (HDU), and referral hospitals requiring advanced clinical positioning.

![Five Function Electric ICU Bed with Trendelenburg in Kenya](/images/blog/5-function-icu-electric-bed-kenya.jpg)

*   **Function:** Five motorized articulating movements:
    1.  Backrest adjustment
    2.  Knee-rest adjustment
    3.  Vertical height adjustment
    4.  Trendelenburg position (head-down tilt)
    5.  Reverse Trendelenburg position (foot-down tilt)
*   **Applications:** Intensive Care Units (ICU), High Dependency Units (HDU), and cardiac care centers.
*   **Key Features:** X-ray translucent backrest board, emergency manual CPR release lever, central braking castor system, integrated side-rail angle indicators, and battery backup.

## 6. Obstetric Delivery & Maternity Bed (3-Function)

## Quick Reference: Hospital Bed Selection Matrix

| Facility Level / Application | Recommended Bed Model | Primary Reason for Choice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 2–3 Dispensary / Outpatient** | Single-Crank Manual | Low cost, space-efficient, suitable for short stays |
| **Level 3–4 General Wards** | Double-Crank Manual | Optimal price-to-comfort ratio, elevation for eating and resting |
| **High-Dependency Wards** | 3-Crank Manual | Height adjustment reduces nurse injury during transfers |
| **Private Wing / Homecare** | 3-Function Electric | Patient independence, zero physical strain on caregivers |
| **ICU / HDU Critical Care** | 5-Function Electric | Trendelenburg, CPR lever, and cardiac chair positioning |
| **Maternity & Labor Rooms** | 3-Function Obstetric | Convertible leg section and fluid-resistant construction |
