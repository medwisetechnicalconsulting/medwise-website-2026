# We Are Attending AKMLSO 2026 in Mombasa For You

The **31st Annual Scientific & Exhibition Conference** hosted by the **Association of Kenya Medical Laboratory Scientific Officers (AKMLSO)** takes place at **PrideInn Flamingo, Mombasa** from **October 13–16, 2026**.

For healthcare directors, laboratory managers, and lead pathologists running facilities in Kisumu, Eldoret, Nairobi, and across Western Kenya, taking extended leave from clinical operations to travel to the coast is not always feasible.

**Medwise Technical Consulting is participating in AKMLSO 2026 on your behalf.**

Our objective on the exhibition floor is to serve as your operational bridge—evaluating high-performance diagnostic machinery, establishing direct manufacturer channels, and negotiating commercial terms that benefit clinical laboratories long after the conference concludes.

## Why Medwise Is Attending the 31st AKMLSO Conference

As an independent healthcare advisory firm, our biomedical team engages with major global medical device manufacturers and local distributors attending the conference. Rather than viewing the exhibition as a standard industry gathering, we leverage this platform to advocate for regional clinical laboratories.

Here is how our presence directly supports your laboratory's operational and growth roadmap:

### 1. Direct Manufacturer & Distributor Partnerships
We negotiate directly with primary diagnostic equipment vendors to secure reliable supply chains, shorter delivery turnarounds, and dedicated biomedical after-sales support for facilities in Western Kenya and beyond.

### 2. Commercial Pricing & Reagent Terms
By acting as an advisory partner for multiple facilities, we leverage collective buying power to negotiate better baseline prices on fully automated analyzers, point-of-care testing (POCT) kits, and daily laboratory consumables.

### 3. Objective Evaluation of Laboratory Innovations
We evaluate emerging diagnostic platforms, benchtop equipment, and AI-assisted analyzer workflows directly on the floor—analyzing true cost-per-test metrics, throughput capacity, and open versus closed reagent compatibility.

### 4. Tracking Regulatory Compliance & Industry Standards
Our team monitors updates regarding laboratory quality management systems, regulatory policies from the **Kenya Medical Laboratory Technicians and Technologists Board (KMLTTB)**, and official licensing workflows accessible via the [eCitizen Services Portal](https://ecitizen.go.ke). Attendees registering directly for the conference can access updates via the official [AKMLSO Portal Registration Page](https://akmlso.or.ke/regi).
