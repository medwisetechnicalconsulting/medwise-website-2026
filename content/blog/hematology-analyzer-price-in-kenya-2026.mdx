## Why Price Varies So Much: 5 Things You Must Check Before You Buy

A cheap analyzer can become the most expensive if you ignore this:

### 1. Open vs Closed Reagent System
Mindray BC-20s and Zybio Z3 are open - you can use third-party reagents (KES 6,500 / set). Sysmex and some 5-part Mindray are closed - you MUST buy original reagents (KES 18,000 - 28,000 / set). Over 1 year, reagent cost is more than the machine price.

### 2. New vs Refurbished (Reconditioned)
Refurbished is not bad. A refurbished Mindray BC-30s from Medwise comes with 6 months warranty, new tubing, new O-rings, calibrated and QC passed. It will serve you 5+ years. But don't buy a refurbished machine that has no service sticker or QC print. That's a scrap.

### 3. Does Price Include KMLTTB Documents?
KMLTTB now asks for equipment validation certificate, calibration certificate and service agreement. Some cheap suppliers dump the machine and disappear. Medwise gives you all audit papers on delivery.

### 4. After-Sales Service Cost
Ask the supplier: "Who will service it after 3 months when WBC clog starts?" If they don't have a biomedical engineer in Kisumu, Nairobi, Mombasa, you will pay KES 15,000 for transport alone per service. We offer service contracts from KES 8,500/quarter.

### 5. Throughput & Hidden Features
Does it have autoloader? How many samples per hour? BC-20s is 40T/H, BC-5380 is 60T/H with autoloader. For a busy Level 4, slow speed means long queues.
